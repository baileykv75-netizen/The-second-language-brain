# Speaking Case

## Source Material

### Source Fact

This is a migrated article-practice session. The original article link was not stored, so this case records only the user's summary and speaking position.

### My Summary

Recovered from a previous IELTS speaking session.

## IELTS Speaking Question

Should universities use potentially harmful chemicals when they are useful for research?

## My Initial Position

I think it depends on the purpose and the level of risk.

## Challenge And Reasoning

### Counterargument

Could a strict safety rule make a harmful chemical acceptable, or should universities always choose the least harmful alternative?

### Final Position

Essential research may justify controlled chemical use, but universities should stop using chemicals that harm people or contaminate waterways and should actively seek safer alternatives.

## Speaking Answers

### 30 Seconds

Whether harmful chemicals should be used on a university campus depends on their purpose and how strictly they are controlled. If a chemical is essential for laboratory research and safety rules are followed, its use may be acceptable. However, if it harms people's health or contaminates waterways, the university should stop using it and look for safer alternatives. Technological development can come at a cost when environmental risks are ignored, so universities, students, and researchers should all play a role in protecting the environment.
