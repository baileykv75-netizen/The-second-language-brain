# Speaking Case

## Source Material

### Source Fact

A woman contacted a computer repair service because her computer problem was urgent and was getting worse. A technician was put in charge of the situation. The computer data had not been backed up, so there was a risk of losing it. The source does not establish whether the delay was personally caused by the technician or whether the company had a broader service failure.

### My Summary

A woman wanted a technician to repair her computer. She was dissatisfied because the service had not responded quickly, the computer problem was serious, and the data had not been backed up. The technician had been put in charge of the case, although the source did not prove that the entire delay was his personal fault.

## IELTS Speaking Question

Do you think the woman's anger was reasonable? Why or why not?

## My Initial Position

Both sides were understandable. The woman had a real reason to worry because her computer was not working properly. At the same time, the technician was following the company's instructions, so the whole problem might not have been his personal responsibility.

## Challenge And Reasoning

### Counterargument

The claim that the problem had nothing to do with the technician was too strong. The source says that he had been put in charge, so he did have some professional responsibility. However, the source did not prove that he personally caused the delay.

### Final Position

The woman's anger was probably reasonable because she was dealing with an urgent problem, the technician had been put in charge, and the data had not been backed up. However, it is uncertain whether her anger should have been directed entirely at that individual technician rather than at the company or its service process.

## Speaking Answers

### 30 Seconds

The woman was probably right to be upset. She was dealing with an urgent problem, and the technician had been put in charge of the situation. In addition, the computer data had not been backed up, so there was a real risk of losing important information. However, we cannot be certain that the delay was entirely the technician's fault.
