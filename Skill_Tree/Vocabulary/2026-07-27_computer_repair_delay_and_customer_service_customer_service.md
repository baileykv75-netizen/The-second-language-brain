# Customer service

Meaning:
The help and support a company provides before, during, or after a purchase or service.

Pronunciation:
customer /ˈkʌs.tə.mər/

Example:
Good customer service requires companies to respond quickly and solve problems clearly.

IELTS usage:
Useful for business, shopping, services, and workplace topics.
