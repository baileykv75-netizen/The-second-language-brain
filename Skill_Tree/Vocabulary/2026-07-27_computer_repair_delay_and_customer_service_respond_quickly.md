# Respond quickly

Meaning:
To react or reply without unnecessary delay.

Pronunciation:
respond /rɪˈspɒnd/

Example:
Customers expect service companies to respond quickly when a problem is urgent.

IELTS usage:
Useful for customer service, communication, public services, and business questions.
