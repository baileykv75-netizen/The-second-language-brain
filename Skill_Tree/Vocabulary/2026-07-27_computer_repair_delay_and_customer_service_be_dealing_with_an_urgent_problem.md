# Be dealing with an urgent problem

Meaning:
To be facing a problem that requires immediate attention.

Pronunciation:
urgent /ˈɜː.dʒənt/

Example:
She was dealing with an urgent problem, so a slow response made her more anxious.

IELTS usage:
Useful for customer service, work, emergencies, and problem-solving questions.

Related words:
pressing issue, immediate attention, emergency
