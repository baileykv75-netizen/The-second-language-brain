# Be put in charge

Meaning:
To be given responsibility for a person, task, or situation.

Pronunciation:
charge /tʃɑːdʒ/

Example:
The technician had been put in charge of the situation.

IELTS usage:
Useful for work, leadership, responsibility, and service questions.

Related words:
be responsible for, manage, handle
